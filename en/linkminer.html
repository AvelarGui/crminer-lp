Frequently asked questions

Before your first link.

The essentials to start now.

What is LinkMiner?

It is a dedicated page to bring together products, services, content, contacts and important links for your brand.

What's the difference for a links page?

In addition to organizing your destinations, LinkMiner can transform an interaction configured for capture into a contact with origin and interest registered in crminer.

Can I use it in my Instagram bio?

Yes. You can share the LinkMiner address in your bio and on other channels.

Can I post products and services?

Yes. Products, services, content, contacts, offers and coupons can be presented on the page.

How does LinkMiner turn interest into opportunity?

At points configured for capture, the visitor provides their data and the context of the interaction can reach the crminer.

Do I need to use all of crminer?

Not necessarily. LinkMiner can be an entry point for your operation, and you can evolve its use according to your needs.

Can I use QR Code with LinkMiner?

Yes. The page address can be used in QR Codes and other materials.

Do I need to install any applications?

There is no need to install an app to access a LinkMiner page.

Is LinkMiner free?

Yes. You can create your LinkMiner for free, without a credit card.
